# CTI Role in Network Defense (§20.01)

> **LO#01: Understand the role of CTI in network defense** _(Mod 20 p4)_
> Covers pp4–8.

## Definition

- **CTI = collection and analysis of information about threats and adversaries** → **informed decisions** on **preparedness, prevention, response** _(Mod 20 p5)_
- **Drawing patterns**; recognizing/discovering **"unknown threats"** so defenses apply before impact _(Mod 20 p5)_
- Collecting, researching, analyzing **trends and technical developments** — cybercrime, hacktivism, espionage _(Mod 20 p5)_
- **Any knowledge about threats that helps plans/decisions = threat intelligence**; aim = **proactive posture** before threats hit _(Mod 20 p5)_
- **Unknown → known** conversion; flags risk factors for **malware, SQL injection, web-app attacks, data leaks, phishing, DoS** → checklist _(Mod 20 p5)_
- Predicts future attacks via **mechanisms + implications** insight; readiness status; **attack-surface reduction**; visibility of threats and risk to security infrastructure _(Mod 20 p4)_
- Presented as **indicators**; **IOCs + IOAs = evidence-based knowledge** of the org's unique threat landscape; different TI sources collect them; relayed to the org ("these indictors" sic) _(Mod 20 p5)_
- Lets defenders grasp **what an attacker is doing + how to stop/prevent it** _(Mod 20 p5)_

## Why orgs consume CTI

Three printed purposes _(Mod 20 p6)_:

- **Defend** information systems, data, network
- **Forward-leaning strategic, operational, tactical decisions** on existing/emerging threats
- **Identify/mitigate business risks** — unknown → known → **advanced proactive defense strategies**

Enhances **network security, incident response, risk management**; guides **which security controls** to incorporate _(Mod 20 p6)_ — see [[MOC-Module-18]].

## Objectives

- **Enhanced/automated incident prevention** — consume + analyze external TI → improve internal controls; proactive **strategic, operational, tactical, technical** choices _(Mod 20 p6)_
- **Automation of security operations + remediation** — guides **cybersecurity-investigation decisions**, people + process focus _(Mod 20 p6)_
- **Guidance to cybersecurity activities** — TI center/service monitors smaller sections _(Mod 20 p6)_
- **Improved risk management** — **efficiency** of the process, metrics, mitigation strategies _(Mod 20 p7)_
- **Improved incident detection** — malware systems catch **malicious files**; extract **IOCs, threat actors, TTPs** to find internal threats _(Mod 20 p7)_

## Identify / Detect / Respond / Recover

- **Identify and protect** — internal + external monitoring reveals unknowns/vulns; **adapt strategy to attacker TTPs**; prepared assessment tests ability to operationalize TI _(Mod 20 p7)_
- **Detect** — real-time monitoring → **rapid, efficient** detection; early-stage focus; **fewer irrelevant/false-positive alerts**; reliable feeds uncover **hidden intrusions** _(Mod 20 p7)_
- **Respond** — contextual info (**IOCs, TTPs**) → stop propagation, cut impact/duration, mitigations; better IR decisions _(Mod 20 p7)_ — see [[MOC-Module-16]]
- **Recover** — **detects and removes persistent mechanisms** (e.g. **malicious files installed on systems**) → rapid, efficient recovery _(Mod 20 p8)_
- Compliance + **prioritized security investment** → stronger existing mechanisms _(Mod 20 p8)_
