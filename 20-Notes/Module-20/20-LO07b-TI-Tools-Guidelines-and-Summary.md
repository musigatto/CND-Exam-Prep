## Guidelines for applying AI to TI _(Mod 20 pp83–84)_

- **Use AI tools proactively**: guide to **identify vulnerabilities and exploits**; categorize **risky behaviors and events**; early detection and better response; include in **automated response** for **attack-flow prediction and countermeasure prescription**; acknowledge vectors via tool deployment; **restrict access controls and permissions** against known attacks; **patch vulnerable systems**. _(Mod 20 p83)_
- **Integrate with existing security tools**: TI is **less effective when used alone**; embed in automated systems to recognize **suspicious and malicious activities**; combine with **SIEM via proactive alerting, prioritizing, contextual data**; also use for **WAF, NGFW, endpoint security**. _(Mod 20 p83)_
- **Maintain and improve alert quality**: overwhelmed teams **cannot review every alarm**; analysts **reject signals** with too many **false positives**; AI **categorizes and prioritizes alerts and eliminates false positives** by comparing against **feeds and databases**; prevents **alert fatigue**, ensures **higher-priority issues first**. _(Mod 20 p84)_
- **Transparent and accountable**: transparency = understand **what data is generated by AI vs humans**; accountability = **shared responsibility between vendors/creators and deployers**. _(Mod 20 p84)_
- **Secure and resilient**: prioritize **physical assets, technologies, sensitive information**; assure via the **CIA triad per organizational priority**; resilience = **tolerance to withstand disasters and cyberattacks plus time to recover**. _(Mod 20 p84)_
- **Supervised to avoid biased results**: models may **reflect biases**; such data is unfit for **vital analytical operations**; mitigate by **improving diversity, mitigating diversity deficits, supervised learning algorithms, general awareness tools**. _(Mod 20 p84)_

## Module Summary _(Mod 20 p85)_

- **CTI** = **collection and analysis of information about threats and adversaries** for informed **preparedness, prevention, and response** decisions. _(Mod 20 p85)_
- CTI lets defenders **understand what an attacker is doing and how to stop or prevent an attack**. _(Mod 20 p85)_
- **IOCs** help prevent **repeated and unchanged threats**; **IOAs** help detect **new and modified threats**. _(Mod 20 p85)_
- An **intelligence provider** can be an **open-source community or movement** or a **private or commercial body** providing **sources, feeds, platforms, and professional services**. _(Mod 20 p85)_
- Define the **goals, need, and purpose before consuming TI**. _(Mod 20 p85)_
