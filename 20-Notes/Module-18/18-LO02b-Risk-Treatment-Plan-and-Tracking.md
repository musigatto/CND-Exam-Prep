# Risk Treatment, Plan, Tracking and Review (LO#02, pp19–24)

> **LO#02: Learn to manage risk through a risk management program**
> Covers pp19–24. Sibling: [[18-LO02a-Risk-Context-Identification-Analysis]] precedes (context, identification, analysis).

## Risk treatment — definition _(Mod 18 p19)_

- **Selecting and implementing appropriate controls** on identified risks **in order to modify them**; risks treated **according to severity level**.
- Decisions **based on the results of a risk assessment**.
- Identifies treatments for risks **outside the department's risk tolerance** (source wording: department's); sets **priority order** to treat, monitor, review.
- Before treating, gather: **appropriate method; users responsible; costs; benefits; likelihood of success; ways to measure and assess treatment**.
- Then **develop and regularly review the risk management plan**.

## P19 four-option wording _(Mod 18 p19)_

| Option | Printed meaning |
|---|---|
| Avoiding the risk | Avoiding the activities that lead to increased risk probability |
| Reducing the risk | Reducing the likelihood of occurrence and reducing the impact if it occurs |
| Transferring the risk | Shifting responsibilities to another party through insurance or partnership |
| Accepting the risk | If it cannot be avoided or transferred |

## Actions to minimize or eliminate risk _(Mod 18 p20)_

- Develop a risk control plan; find the impact of risk control on service delivery.
- Constraints for risk control are identified and considered when completing the plan.
- Implementation of risk control strategies.
- Quoted verbatim (no further detail in source): `Uncontrollable risks`; `Client resistance to risk control`.
- Communicate with support workers / other workers during risk control.
- **Completely document the risk control plan** as part of the process.

## Risk treatment process — six printed options _(Mod 18 p21)_

| Option | Printed definition |
|---|---|
| Eliminate the Risk | Applying controls to reduce the threat of exploiting the vulnerability to zero |
| Transfer the Risk | Transferring the risk factor to a third party that can manage the risk levels (figure: transferring treatment responsibility to another party or organization) |
| Mitigate the Risk | Reducing risks of a threat or vulnerability by implementing direct or competing controls |
| Accept the Risk | Risk factor at an acceptable level; accepted when efforts to address, transfer, or mitigate exceed impact on the network |
| Risk Avoidance | Avoiding the factor that enhances the risk of a business process, or an alternative fitting business needs — e.g. not allowing laptops to avoid associated risks |
| Reduce the Risk | Reducing the likelihood rate to an acceptable level — e.g. implementing safety controls |

- Steps differ by case; **stakeholders and process owners mutually decide**.
- Can **change the likelihood of risk occurrence**.

## Treatment considerations incl. residual risk _(Mod 18 pp21–22)_

- Implement an appropriate risk treatment option.
- Ensure **adequate resources** for implementation.
- Plan must reduce the risk factor to an **acceptable level**.
- Take **remedial actions** for risks needing immediate handling.
- **Note: options do not always mitigate completely — residual risks persist** and must be considered.

## Risk treatment plan _(Mod 18 p23)_

- **Action plan describing how to respond to potential risks**; summary of identified risks, each designed response, parties responsible, **target date** for treatment.
- **Essential document of a certified ISO 27001 information security management system.**
- Figure contents: **proposed security controls with priorities and deadlines; required resources; roles and responsibilities of stakeholders; performance; reporting and monitoring requirements**.
- Developing it requires determining the **treatment level at each risk level** (e.g. moderate, minor, high) and improvement opportunities to offset risks.
- To implement correctly and monitor accurately, ensure: right structure supports the plan; adequate resources for those mitigating; communication within the plan and with key stakeholders; **accurate and timely risk analysis**; owner specifies how implementation is monitored, **including increasing or decreasing risk levels**; plan **routinely reviewed** for effectiveness and risk levels.

## Risk tracking and review _(Mod 18 p24)_

- Well-planned and **regular monitoring and review** required to identify new risks and reduce them.
- **Tracking:** identifies the chance of a **new** risk; monitors **probability, impact, status, exposure**; identified risks regularly reviewed, changes documented — e.g. evaluation modified when risk-reducing controls are implemented and new risks recorded.
- **Review:** evaluates **effectiveness** of implemented strategies; finds shortcomings, enhances controls; maintains objectives and keeps context up-to-date and accurate.
- **Risk reporting keeps management aware of the top risks** so they can plan reductions.
- Related: assessed risks materialize as incidents — [[MOC-Module-16]].
