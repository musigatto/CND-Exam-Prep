## COBIT framework

- **COBIT** is an **IT governance framework and supporting toolset** that allows managers to **bridge the gap between control requirements, technical issues and business risks**. _(Mod 18 p35)_
- Emphasizes **regulatory compliance**, helps increase value from IT, enables alignment, simplifies implementation of enterprise IT governance and control framework. _(Mod 18 p35)_
- Source: `http://www.isaca.org` _(Mod 18 p35)_
- **Control Objectives for Information and Related Technologies (COBIT)** — framework designed by **ISACA** for governance and management of enterprise information (all technology and information processing the enterprise establishes to achieve its goals, regardless of where this occurs) and technology, aimed at the whole enterprise. _(Mod 18 p35)_
- COBIT helps enterprises: **maintain high-quality information** to support business decisions; **achieve strategic goals** and realize business benefits through effective and innovative use of IT; **achieve operational excellence** through reliable and efficient application of technology; **maintain IT-related risk at an acceptable level**; **optimize cost** of IT services and technology; **support compliance** with relevant laws, regulations, policies. _(Mod 18 p35)_

## Governance conceptual model

- Governance System Principles: **six principles** are the core requirements for a governance system for enterprise information and technology. _(Mod 18 p36)_
- Provide Stakeholders Value: each enterprise needs a governance system to **satisfy stakeholder needs and generate value from IT**. _(Mod 18 p36)_
- Holistic Approach: governance system built from components of different types that **work together holistically**. _(Mod 18 p36)_
- Dynamic Governance System: each time one or more design factors are changed, consider the impact on the governance system. _(Mod 18 p36)_
- Governance Distinct from Management: governance system should clearly distinguish between the two (sentence truncated in source). _(Mod 18 p36)_
- Governance Framework Principles: **Based on Conceptual Model** — identify key components and relationships among components to maximize consistency and allow automation; **Open and Flexible** — allow new content and new issues while maintaining integrity and consistency; **Aligned to Major Standards** — align to relevant major standards, frameworks, regulations. _(Mod 18 p37)_
- Governance or management objective always relates to **one process** plus related components of other types; governance objective relates to a governance process, management objective to a management process. _(Mod 18 p37)_
- Goals Cascade: enterprise goals are consolidated, reduced, updated, clarified; thereafter alignment goals emphasize **alignment of all IT efforts with business objectives**. _(Mod 18 p37)_
- Components of a Governance System: built from components of different types that interact, resulting in a holistic governance system for IT. _(Mod 18 p37)_
- Focus Area: a governance topic, domain, or issue addressed by a collection of governance and management objectives and components; can mix generic and variant components; number virtually unlimited, making COBIT open-ended; new focus areas added as required or by expert contribution. _(Mod 18 p37)_

## Enterprise network risk management policy

- Written statement to **protect organizational assets from accidental or malicious threats**; include network risk management policies that comply with organizational security policies. _(Mod 18 p38)_
- Assists developing and establishing essential processes and procedures to **address and minimize information security risks**; outlines aspects of risk and identifies people to manage risk. _(Mod 18 p38)_
- Addresses infosec issues and impact; suggests measures to secure assets from **internal and external risks**. _(Mod 18 p38)_
- Objectives: **equip the organization** with skills to identify and treat risks; **provide a consistent RMF**; **provide overall direction and purpose** of risk management; **manage risks** with adequate mitigation techniques; **combat existing and emerging risks**; **integrate operational risks** into the process; **accomplish strategic and operational goals**; **assist strategic management decisions**; **meet legal and regulatory requirements**. _(Mod 18 p38)_

## Best practices for implementation

- Track and monitor internal and external risks at regular intervals. _(Mod 18 p39)_
- Establish a risk management policy; implement a framework for risk assessment and mapping. _(Mod 18 p39)_
- Use ERM for decision-making; incorporate ERM into the strategic-planning process. _(Mod 18 p39)_
- Identify potential risks to the network; create a common language and reporting system for communicating KRIs. _(Mod 18 p39)_
- Prioritize risks based on impact on the enterprise network; specify responsibilities for risk management with respective domains; regularly review and update the policy. _(Mod 18 p39)_
- Identify threats and risks from user errors; analyze risks in normal and fault conditions. _(Mod 18 p39)_
- Risk assessment by experienced and trained professionals; identify risk in its initial stage for quick response; choose proper metrics to measure effectiveness. _(Mod 18 p39)_
