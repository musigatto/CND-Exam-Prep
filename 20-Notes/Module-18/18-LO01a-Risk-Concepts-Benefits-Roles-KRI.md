# Risk Concepts, Benefits, Roles, KRI and KPI (LO#01)

> **LO#01: Understand risk management concepts** _(Mod 18 pp4–11)_
> Covers pp4–11. Section intro: this section introduces risk management concepts, key risk indicators (KRIs), key roles, and responsibilities in risk management _(Mod 18 p5)_.

> "Risk and vulnerability management is a pro-active approach to manage network security" _(Mod 18 p4)_

## Risk management — definitions

- **Process of reducing and maintaining risk at an acceptable level** by means of a **well-defined and actively employed security program** _(Mod 18 p6)_.
- Involves **identifying, assessing, and responding to the risks** by implementing controls to help the organization manage the potential effects _(Mod 18 p6)_.
- Has a **prominent place throughout the system security life-cycle**; continuous and increasingly complex; requires **anticipating risks and creating a plan** to overcome the risk when it occurs _(Mod 18 p6)_.
- Type of risks varies by organization, but **all organizations should prepare a management plan**; helps **save time, money, and efforts** _(Mod 18 p6)_.

## Objectives

- **Identify the potential risks** _(Mod 18 p6)_.
- Identify the **impact of risks**; help the organization develop better risk management strategies and plans _(Mod 18 p6)_.
- Depending on **impact/severity**, **prioritize** the risks and use established risk management methods, tools, and techniques _(Mod 18 p6)_.
- **Understand and analyze** the risks and **report identified risk events** _(Mod 18 p6)_.
- **Control the risk and mitigate the risk impact** _(Mod 18 p6)_.
- Create awareness among security staff; develop **long-term, reliable strategies and plans** _(Mod 18 p6)_.

## Benefits (7, printed order)

1. Focuses on **potential risk impact areas** _(Mod 18 pp6–7)_.
2. Addresses risks **according to the risk level** _(Mod 18 pp6–7)_.
3. Improves the **risk handling process** _(Mod 18 pp6–7)_.
4. Allows security officers to **act effectively in adverse situations** _(Mod 18 pp6–7)_.
5. Enables **effective use of risk handling resources** _(Mod 18 pp6–7)_.
6. **Minimizes the effect of risk on the organization's revenue** _(Mod 18 pp6–7)_.
7. Identifies **suitable controls for security** _(Mod 18 pp6–7)_.
- Framing benefit: provides a **structured approach to identifying risks**; a clear idea of all risks lets the organization **analyze, prioritize, and take appropriate actions to reduce losses** _(Mod 18 p6)_.

## KRI

- A **key risk indicator (KRI)** is an important component of an effective risk management process that **shows the riskiness of an activity**; **understanding organizational goals** is required to identify KRIs _(Mod 18 p10)_.
- A KRI is a **metric showing the risk appetite probability** for an organization _(Mod 18 p10)_.
- KRIs indicate riskiness **at an early stage**; the most important indicators of overall health; **reduce loss and prevent risk exposure** by measuring **risk profiles and risk situations in advance** before the event occurs _(Mod 18 p10)_.
- Slide triad: **define risk for an objective; identify the possibility of adverse effect; send early warning** to identify a potential adverse event _(Mod 18 p10)_.
- Role of KRIs _(Mod 18 p10)_:
  - Identify **current risk exposure and emerging trends**; early warning and proactive action.
  - **Event impact identification**; **threshold level notifications**.
  - **Backward looking view**, enabling learning lessons from past events.
  - Highlight **weaknesses of existing controls**; strengthen poor controls.
  - Facilitate **risk-reporting and escalation**.
  - Indicate that **risk appetite and tolerance are reached**.
  - Provide **real-time actionable intelligence** to decision-makers and risk managers.
- Features of effective KRIs _(Mod 18 pp10–11)_:
  - **Quantifiable metrics** — measurable (number, count, or percentage).
  - **Predictable** — early warning signals.
  - **Comparable** — trackable over a period of time.
  - **Informational** — measure the status of the risk and control.

## KPI and KRI development

- KRIs should accurately measure and reflect any **negative impact on key performance indicators (KPI)** _(Mod 18 p11)_.
- **KPI is a metric that assesses the progress of an organization toward its goals**, and provides **leading indicator information about emerging risks from external events** that impact demand for products or services _(Mod 18 p11)_.
- KRIs represent **key ratios** tracked as indicators of **evolving risks and potential opportunities**; guide responses _(Mod 18 p11)_.
- KRIs should be **reported regularly**; proper **escalation methods and plans** enable timely reporting; KRIs have **different escalation levels**; management maps risks to identify KRIs for strategic initiatives _(Mod 18 p11)_.
- Development method: first **identify risk events** that could impact financial status, then find the **intermediate and root cause**; the indicator assists management in **responding in advance** _(Mod 18 p11)_.
