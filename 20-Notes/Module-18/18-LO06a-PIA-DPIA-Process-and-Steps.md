> **LO#06: Discuss Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA)** _(Mod 18 p3)_

## DPIA — structured process _(Mod 18 p66)_

- **DPIA = data protection impact assessment** — a **structured and systematic approach** to **assess and manage privacy risks** of specific data processing activities. _(Mod 18 p66)_
- Essential for compliance with regulations such as **GDPR**; designed to **protect the rights and freedoms** of individuals whose personal data is processed. _(Mod 18 p66)_

6. **Identify measures to mitigate risk** — suggest mitigation measures to the identified **risks and hams** [sic]; determine **effect and severity of risk** on assets. _(Mod 18 p67)_

## PIA — definition _(Mod 18 p68)_

- **PIA = privacy impact assessment** — a **risk assessment procedure** examining processes that address **data protection risks in an information system**; identifies privacy risks to confidential information. _(Mod 18 p68)_
- Offers insights into deficiencies in **privacy, compliance, risk management**; prevents or mitigates risks, enhancing privacy protections. _(Mod 18 p68)_
- Explains **purpose of collecting PII, how it is maintained, protected, retained**. _(Mod 18 p68)_

## PIA — three primary objectives _(Mod 18 p68)_

1. **Ensure adherence** to relevant legal, regulatory, policy requirements for privacy.
2. **Identify and address** risks of privacy breaches and their effects.
3. **Assess alternative processes and protections** to mitigate potential privacy risks. _(Mod 18 p68)_

## PIA is conducted when _(Mod 18 p68)_

- **New technologies** adapted/developed to handle **PII**.
- **Designing fresh data/information** that could pose heavy risk.
- **Updating a system** that could introduce new risks.
- **Implementing/revising rulemaking** involving PII gathering. _(Mod 18 p68)_

## PIA process — 12 steps _(Mod 18 pp69–70)_

1. **Preliminary Assessment** — identify projects/systems/processes collecting, processing, sharing personal data; consider **data sensitivity, scale**. _(Mod 18 p69)_
2. **Form the PIA Team** — expertise in **data protection, legal, security, compliance**, plus the project under evaluation. _(Mod 18 p69)_
3. **Data Mapping and Description** — document **data types, purposes, sources, flows, recipients**. _(Mod 18 p69)_
4. **Data Privacy Compliance Check** — examine applicable laws (e.g. **GDPR**); check processing aligns with legal requirements. _(Mod 18 p69)_
5. **Risk Assessment** — analyze processing for privacy risks; consider **data sensitivity, access controls, retention, impact on rights and freedoms**. _(Mod 18 p70)_
6. **Privacy Impact Identification** — evaluate effect on individuals, incl. **discrimination, harm, violations of data subjects' rights**. _(Mod 18 p70)_
7. **Risk Mitigation Measures** — formulate technical/organizational safeguards such as **encryption, access controls, anonymization**. _(Mod 18 p70)_
8. **Documentation** — document process, findings, mitigation measures, consultations; essential for **transparency, accountability, compliance**. _(Mod 18 p70)_
9. **Consultation** — if high privacy risks, engage **stakeholders, data protection authorities, data subjects**. _(Mod 18 p70)_
10. **Report and Approval** — formal report reviewed/approved by **stakeholders, DPO, legal, management**. _(Mod 18 p70)_
11. **Integration and Implementation** — implement mitigation measures into the project/system/process. _(Mod 18 p70)_
12. **Ongoing Monitoring and Review** — continuously monitor/review effectiveness; **periodic review** for changes in processing or privacy requirements; not a one-time exercise. _(Mod 18 p70)_

## Importance of PIA in risk management _(Mod 18 p71)_

- **Critical component of the risk management framework**; helps **identify, assess, mitigate privacy risks**. _(Mod 18 p71)_
- Integrates privacy into projects/processes → **reduces likelihood of privacy incidents/data breaches**, protects **rights and freedoms of data subjects**, assures **compliance**. _(Mod 18 p71)_
- Provides **evidence a compliance standard requires**; promotes **privacy-by-design** from the outset; creates/implements **privacy controls and mitigation strategies**. _(Mod 18 p71)_
