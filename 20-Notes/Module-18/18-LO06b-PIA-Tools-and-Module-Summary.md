## PIA vs Privacy Risk Assessment _(Mod 18 p77)_

- **PIA** — approach identifying and reducing risks to users', clients', stakeholders' personal information; **mandatory if a project involves personal information**; conducted by **sub-agencies or third-party tools**; investigates risks of collection, use, dissemination, management of **PII**. _(Mod 18 p77)_
- **Privacy risk assessment** — framework evaluating risks of **PII**; **PIA and DPIA classified under privacy risk management**; mandatory prior to certain tasks; conducted by the **security team**; management board members responsible in their areas. _(Mod 18 p77)_
- Acts as an **early warning system** detecting privacy problems, improving internal information availability, avoiding costly compliance mistakes; prevents legal implications of non-compliance; assessments conducted and **managed internally**. _(Mod 18 p77)_

## Module Summary _(Mod 18 p78)_

- **Risk management** has a prominent place in securing the network lifecycle; process of **reducing and maintaining risk at an acceptable level** using a well-defined active security program. _(Mod 18 p78)_
- **KRI** = metric showing risk appetite probability. **Impact level** depends on **asset value and data criticality**. _(Mod 18 p78)_
- Organizations maintain **vulnerability management for their RMFs**; **ERM** defines implementation activities for handling risk. _(Mod 18 p78)_
- **Risk-based vulnerability assessment** identifies, classifies, analyzes vulnerabilities to **mitigate or remediate** them. _(Mod 18 p78)_
