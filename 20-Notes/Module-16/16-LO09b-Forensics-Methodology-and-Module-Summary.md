# Forensics Methodology and Module Summary (§16.09)

> **LO#09: Describe the forensics investigation process** _(Mod 16 p126)_
> Covers pp126–129.

## Typical methodology — 9 steps _(Mod 16 pp126–127)_

1. **Obtain a search warrant** — before investigating suspects; beneficial to the investigator.
2. **Evaluate and secure the scene** — before collecting; tampering/damage affects evidential proof.
3. **Collect the evidence** — all evidence found; **neglect no supporting items** usable in court.
4. **Secure the evidence** — store securely; **loss weakens the case**.
5. **Acquire the data** — the affected data; identifies the **reason for the intrusion**.
6. **Analyze the data** — incl. **monitoring target activity before, during, after**; **most important phase**, more evidence via logs.
7. **Assess the evidence and the case** — after analysis, **gather the evidence and assess it**.
8. **Prepare the final report** — detailed info on **actions of investigator and suspect/attacker**.
9. **Testify as an expert witness** — confirms the **facts of the case**.

Methodology as a whole guides collection so evidence is **admissible in court**. _(Mod 16 p126)_

## Post-analysis flow (running prose, not the figure) _(Mod 16 p128)_

- Team **analyzes evidence** to find **real cause and nature**, traces perpetrators; **documents and submits results to management**.
- Perpetrator identified → management decides **law enforcement prosecution vs organizational disciplinary team**; if law enforcement, designated authority **contacts a third-party agency**.
- Attacker not identified → **close the investigation or pass to an external agency**; third-party findings go back to management for prosecution decisions; if third parties also fail, **IRT/management recommends updating IR processes**.
- **Severe incidents** (employees, customers, public affected; severe damage/financial loss) → **notify external law enforcement/investigation agencies and file a case** (local/national law enforcement, security agencies, cyber experts).

## Module Summary _(Mod 16 p129)_

- **Timely responses prevent major losses**; the **first responder is vital**; the **IRT works from the first responder's initial information**.
- Module covered the full IRT process: **handling, eradication, containment, investigation, recovery** for all incident types.
- **Incident handling and response** = **organized, careful steps**; **quick response minimizes damage**.
- First responder **escalates to the infosec team and the in-house or external IRT**.
- Incidents categorized: **false positive, true positive, false negative, true negative**.
- **Forensics** = identify, gather, preserve, extract, interpret, document, present evidence; **runs simultaneously with containment**.
