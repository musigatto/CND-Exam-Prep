# Forensics Concept and People (§16.09)

> **LO#09: Describe the forensics investigation process** _(Mod 16 p122)_
> Covers pp122–125.

## Definition _(Mod 16 p123)_

- **Forensic investigation** = **methodological procedures and techniques** to **identify, gather, preserve, extract, interpret, document, and present evidence** from incident-affected systems, **acceptable in legal and/or administrative proceedings**.
- **Conducted simultaneously with the containment process**.
- IR contains events; **forensics finds the root cause**. Main goal: **identify the incident, the time, the perpetrator, and steps to mitigate future occurrences**.

## Objectives — 5 _(Mod 16 p123)_

1. **Track and prosecute perpetrators** of cyber crime
2. **Gather evidence in a forensically sound manner**
3. **Estimate potential impact** on the victim and **assess perpetrator intent**
4. **Minimize tangible and intangible losses**
5. **Protect the organization from similar incidents**

## Role of forensic analysis in IR _(Mod 16 p123)_

- Evaluates/investigates **data from before and after the attack period**; determines the **exact cause**; **generates a timeline correlating incidents**.
- **Balances operations vs security** per budget; determines **nature and impact**; **mitigates breach loss, begins recovery**; **tracks attackers**.
- **Extracts, processes, interprets factual evidence** proving attacker actions in court; **damage assessment saves money and time**; saves org from **legal liabilities and lawsuits**.

## Team roles _(Mod 16 pp124–125)_

| Role | What the page says |
|---|---|
| Attorney | **Legal advice** on how the investigation should run + **legal aspects to comply** with |
| Photographer | **Photographs crime scene and evidence**; must be **certified for evidence photography**; photo record = key evidence |
| Incident Responder | Measures taken when incident occurs; **secures the incident area, collects crime-scene evidence** |
| Decision Maker | **Authority for policy/procedure** followed; adapts policies to **incident type and requirements** |
| Incident Analyzer | Analyzes incidents by **occurrence** — type, system effect, **threats and vulnerabilities** |
| Evidence Examiner/Investigator | **Examines acquired evidence, sorts useful evidence by case relevance**; maintains **evidence hierarchy**, prioritizes appropriately |
| Evidence Documenter | **Documents all evidence and every investigation phase**, orderly, **from occurrence to end**; gathers info from **all people involved**; complete forensics record |
| Evidence Manager | Manages evidence so it is **admissible in court**; holds **name, type, time, source** of each item; maintains the record |
| Expert Witness | Offers **formal opinion as testimony in court**; **authenticates facts/witnesses** of complex cases; called to **cross-examine witnesses and evidence** (normal witnesses may be influenced) |
