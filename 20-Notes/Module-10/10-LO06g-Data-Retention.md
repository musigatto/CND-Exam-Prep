# Data Backup Retention (§10.6.7)

## What
- **Data retention** = process of storing and maintaining important information to meet **compliance** and **business data archival** requirements; protects business-critical data from loss
- **Data retention policy** = set of rules preserving + maintaining data for operational/regulatory compliance; defines **required retention periods per data type** and **minimum standards for destroying** certain information
- Applied to: all business units/processes/systems/countries; agents, contractors, consultants; all documents — emails, hard/soft copies, audio/video

## Steps to create a retention policy
1. **Build a development team** — in-house legal counsels, dept managers/supervisors, financial-report staff + staff managing retention settings
2. **Identify applicable regulatory compliances** — HIPAA, SOX, IRS, COPPA, EU GDPR (each sets durations + removal conditions)
3. **Specify data types included** — documents, emails, customer records, transactions, contracts, employee records, invoices, tax/accounting, financial reports, healthcare/patient data, student/educational data, etc.
4. **Develop the policy** — purpose · applicable laws · **record retention + deletion schedule** · litigation plan · review/update schedule
5. **Inform all employees** — provide copies, training + review sessions

## Best practices
- Fulfill **legal + business requirements** · justify policy details · keep it **simple + easy to implement** · start minimal, add as required
- **Different policies for different data types** (per legal/business impact) · retain customer/user info **only as long as necessary**
- **Implement software** to manage retention tasks · **classify data**: archive or delete · move **infrequently accessed files to lower-level archive** · organize archives for easy access
