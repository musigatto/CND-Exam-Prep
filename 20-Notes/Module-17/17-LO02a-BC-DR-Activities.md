# BC/DR Activities — Prevention to Restoration (§17.02)

> **LO#02: Discuss BC/DR activities**
> Covers pp15–18. _(Mod 17 pp15–18)_

The section covers five activities: **prevention, response, resumption, recovery, restoration**. _(Mod 17 p15)_

## 1. Prevention

- Actions to **prevent a natural phenomenon or potential hazard from harming** the organisation. _(Mod 17 p16)_
- Example: **restrictions on processes** — e.g. restrict spending capital on items **not listed in the DRP or BCP**. _(Mod 17 pp16–17)_
- Preventive control strategy **protects critical business areas** and mitigates threat impact; effective mechanisms **do not allow unauthorised access** and cause **no availability problem**. _(Mod 17 p17)_

## 2. Response

- Set of activities **after a disaster** to **assess business needs** and **reduce/limit negative impacts**. _(Mod 17 pp16–17)_
- Examples: **evacuating personnel, shutting down systems**. _(Mod 17 p16)_
- Initial response items: **generating notifications; activating the business continuity team (BCT); activating business unit personnel; initial briefing to the BCT; reviewing recovery strategies for implementation; implementing the BCP**. _(Mod 17 p17)_

## 3. Resumption

- **Recommencement of business operations** after a disruptive incident; robust infrastructure crucial. _(Mod 17 pp16–17)_
- Example: **continuing operations at a primary or alternate location** (alternate used when primary inaccessible/unusable). _(Mod 17 pp16–17)_
- Activates **alternative infrastructure resources** and **time-sensitive business processes**; **cannot resume** in large-scale destruction — consult **emergency operations center** on whether to **invoke the BCP**. _(Mod 17 p17)_
- First decision: resume at **primary location vs shift to alternate site**; if normal site damaged or access denied, shift to alternate. _(Mod 17 p17)_

## 4. Recovery

- Actions to **resume services dependent on critical business applications**. _(Mod 17 p16)_
- Example: **program to restore the disaster site and damaged materials to a stable and usable condition**. _(Mod 17 p16)_

## 5. Restoration

- Repair the **old site** or set up a **completely new alternate site**; restores operations to **normalcy**, often by **migrating business functions from the recovery site to the long-term site**. _(Mod 17 p16)_
- p18 phase detail (prose only; figure not read):
  - Concerned with **repair and restoration of the primary site**; **initiated only in case of physical damage**. _(Mod 17 p18)_
  - Team **assesses physical damage, replaces damaged items, refurbishes premises**, restoring normalcy. _(Mod 17 p18)_
  - Initially **operations team implements DRP/BCP at the alternate site**; then **technical team formulates the restoration plan**. _(Mod 17 p18)_
  - Operations team **splits in two** — one group keeps implementing DRP/BCP, the other manages **restoration at the primary site**; often both run **simultaneously**. _(Mod 17 p18)_

Sequence: prevention → response → resumption → recovery → restoration. _(Mod 17 pp15–18)_
