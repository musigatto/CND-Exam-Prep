# Types of Wireless Technologies (§13.01)

> **LO#01: Understand the fundamentals of wireless networks** _(Mod 13 p4)_
> Covers p8–9.

## The four technologies at a glance _(Mod 13 p8)_

| Technology | IEEE standard | Band | Range / rate | Notes |
|------------|---------------|------|--------------|-------|
| **Wi-Fi** (wireless fidelity) | **802.11** family | **2.4 GHz to 5 GHz** | — | Uses radio waves **or microwaves**; signal path is wireless adapter → antenna and router |
| **Bluetooth** | **802.15** | **2.4 GHz** | up to **10 m**; **less than 1 Mbps** | Uses **FHSS** for data transfer |
| **RFID** (radio-frequency identification) | — (none given) | RF electromagnetic waves | up to **20 ft** | Automatic identification + tracking of tags attached to objects |
| **WiMax** (worldwide interoperability for microwave access) | **802.16** family | — | **several miles**; up to **75 Mbps** | Long-distance wireless networking; fixed wireless + mobile stations |

## Wi-Fi _(Mod 13 p8)_

- *"part of the **IEEE 802.11 family** of wireless networking standards."*
- *"uses **radio waves or microwaves** to allow electronic devices to exchange data or connect to
  the internet."*
- Supported on *"personal computers, laptops, digital cameras, smartphones, etc."*
- *"Wi-Fi operates in the frequency band **between 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz**."*
- Signal path: *"a computer should have a **wireless adapter** to translate the data into radio
  signals and pass them through an **antenna and router**… where the message is decoded and the data
  is sent to the internet or through another network."*
- **Hotspots** — *"areas that have Wi-Fi availability, where users can enable Wi-Fi on their devices
  and connect to the internet through a hotspot."*

## Bluetooth _(Mod 13 p8)_

- Data transmitted between **cell phones, computers, and other networking devices** over short
  distances.
- Range: *"short distances of **up to 10 m** as compared to other modes of wireless communication."*
- Speed: *"**less than 1 Mbps**"*; frequency: *"**2.4 GHz**."*
- *"comes under **IEEE 802.15** and uses a radio technology called **FHSS**."*

## RFID _(Mod 13 p8)_

- *"uses **radio frequency (RF) electromagnetic waves** to transfer data for **automatic
  identification** and for **tracking tags attached to objects**."*
- *"RFID devices work within a small range of **up to 20 ft**."*

## WiMax _(Mod 13 p8)_

- *"uses **long distance wireless networking** and **high-speed internet**."*
- *"belongs to the **IEEE 802.16 family**."*
- *"WiMax signals can function over a distance of **several miles** with data rates reaching up to
  **75 Mbps**."*
- *"uses a **fixed wireless application** and **mobile stations** to provide high-speed data, voice,
  video calls, and internet connectivity to users."*
- *"The **WiMax forum** developed WiMax and states that **nearly 135 countries** have deployed
  **over 455 WiMax networks**."*

## Wired vs. wireless — Table 13.1 _(Mod 13 p9)_

| Attribute | Wired networks | Wireless networks |
|-----------|----------------|-------------------|
| Bandwidth | **High** bandwidth | **Low** bandwidth |
| Bandwidth variation | **Low** | **High** |
| Error rates | **Low** | **High** |
| Security | **More secure** | **Less secure** |
| Equipment dependency | **Less** dependent | **More** dependent |
| Connectivity | **Symmetric** | **Possible asymmetric** |
| Machine power | **High-power** machines | **Low-power** machines |
| Machine resources | **High-resource** machines | **Low-resource** machines |
| Delay | **Low** delay | **High** delay |
| Operation | **Connected** operation | **Disconnected** operation |

## Advantages of a wireless network _(Mod 13 p9)_

- **Accessibility** — devices can be accessed from any location within the coverage area.
- **Flexibility** — devices may be carried from one location to another within the coverage area;
  internet access from any location.
- **Efficiency** — employees access the internet and complete work within the stipulated time; they
  can work on the go and do not require an office.
- **Easy to set up** — *"requires a low cost and less time."*
- **Security** — *"Advanced security features have been employed for the security of wireless
  networks."*
- **Expandable** — *"It is easy to expand the coverage area of a wireless network for a particular
  location."*

## Disadvantages of a wireless network _(Mod 13 p9)_

- **Electromagnetic interference** caused by another network or other devices *"might interrupt the
  network, leading to system failure and slow/lost signals."*
- **Black spots** — *"Some locations are not suitable for wireless networking and are termed as
  black spots where no signals are available."*
