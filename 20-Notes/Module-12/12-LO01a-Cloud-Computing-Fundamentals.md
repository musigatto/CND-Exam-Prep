# Cloud Computing Fundamentals (§12.01)

> **LO#01 — Understand the cloud computing fundamentals**
> Section scope: types of cloud computing services · separation of responsibilities · cloud
> deployment models · NIST reference architecture _(Mod 12 p4)_

## Definition

> Cloud computing is an **on-demand delivery of IT capabilities in which an IT infrastructure and
> applications are provided to subscribers as metered services over a network.** _(Mod 12 p5)_

Three load-bearing words: **on-demand · metered · over a network**.

Examples the courseware gives: **Gmail, Facebook, Dropbox, Salesforce** _(Mod 12 p5)_

## 12 characteristics _(Mod 12 p5–6)_

Order as printed in the p5 figure, gloss from the p5–6 body text.

| # | Characteristic | Courseware gloss |
|---|----------------|------------------|
| 1 | On-demand self service | CSP renders cloud resources (compute power, storage, network) **without human interaction with service providers** |
| 2 | Distributed storage | Better scalability, availability, reliability of data — **but** can involve security and compliance concerns |
| 3 | Broad network access | "Cloud resources are available over the network and can be a…" _(gloss truncated in source)_ |
| 4 | Rapid elasticity | Instant provisioning; scale up/down to demand; resources "seem to be unlimited" and can be bought in the desired quantity |
| 5 | Automated management | Minimizing user involvement speeds processes, cuts labor costs, reduces the possibility of human error |
| 6 | Resource pooling | CSP pools all resources to serve multiple customers in a multi-tenant environment; physical and virtual resources dynamically assigned/reassigned on demand |
| 7 | Measured service | "Pay-per-use" metering — monthly subscription or per usage (storage levels, processing power, bandwidth); provider monitors, controls, reports and charges **with complete transparency** |
| 8 | Virtualization technology | Enables rapid scaling of resources that non-virtualized environments cannot achieve |
| 9 | Multi-tenancy | Supports multiple tenants on a single set of shared resources |
| 10 | Resilient computing | Architected with **redundancy and fault tolerance as primary considerations** → elevated availability and dependability |
| 11 | Flexible pricing models | Pay-per-use, subscription-based, and spot pricing alternatives |
| 12 | Sustainability | Energy-efficient data centres + renewable energy → mitigate ecological footprint |

> Distinguish #6 resource pooling (provider-side, many customers) from #9 multi-tenancy
> (shared resource set serving multiple tenants) — the courseware lists both. _(Mod 12 p6)_

## Limitations of cloud computing _(Mod 12 p6)_

| # | Limitation |
|---|------------|
| 1 | Organizations have limited control and flexibility |
| 2 | Prone to outage and other technical issues |
| 3 | Security, privacy, and compliance issues |
| 4 | Contracts and lock-ins |
| 5 | Dependence on network connections |

## Benefits — 4 groups, 30 items _(Mod 12 p7–8)_

**Economic** _(Mod 12 p7)_
Business agility · Less maintenance costs · Acquire economies of scale · Less capital
expenditure · Huge storage facilities for organizations · Environment friendly · Less total cost
of ownership · Less power consumption

**Operational** _(p7–8)_
Flexibility and efficiency · Resilience and redundancy · Scale as required · Less operational
problems · Deploy applications quickly · Backup and disaster recovery · Automatic updates

**Staffing** _(Mod 12 p8)_
Streamline processes · Efficient usage of resources · Less personnel training · Less IT staff ·
Multiple users can utilize cloud resources · Evolution of new business models · Simultaneous
sharing of resources

**Security** _(Mod 12 p8)_
Less investment in security controls · Efficient, effective, and swift response to security
breaches · Standardized open interface for managed security services (MSS) · Effective patch
management and implementation of security updates · Better disaster recovery preparedness ·
Ability to dynamically scale defensive resources on demand · Resource aggregation offers better
management of security systems · Rigorous internal audits and risk assessment procedures
