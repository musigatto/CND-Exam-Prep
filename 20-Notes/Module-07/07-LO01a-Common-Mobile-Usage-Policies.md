# Common Mobile Usage Policies (§7.1)

## Four Mobile Use Approaches in Enterprise
Organizations follow **4 approaches** to grant employees permission to use mobile devices for business purposes:

| Policy | Name | Core idea |
|---|---|---|
| **BYOD** | Bring Your Own Device | Employee's personal device used for work |
| **COPE** | Company Owned, Personally Enabled | Org-purchased device, personal use allowed |
| **COBO** | Company Owned, Business Only | Org-purchased device, business use only |
| **CYOD** | Choose Your Own Device | Employee picks from org-approved device list |

## Choosing the Approach — Decision Questions
- **Device specific:** device type (smartphone/phablet/laptop) · who selects/uses which device · who pays for the device · service providers for cellular connectivity + monthly plans
- **Management and support:** who manages the device · who is responsible for support
- **Integration and application:** how closely the device is integrated into the daily workflow · installed/running applications · should personal applications be restricted?
